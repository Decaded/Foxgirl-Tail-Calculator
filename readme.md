# Kitsune Calculator

A simple web tool that calculates the physical properties of kitsune (foxgirls) using real fox anatomy, the golden ratio, and ancient folklore. Input height, age, and tail
preferences to discover detailed measurements of tails, ears, and body proportions of your foxgirl character.

## The Science Behind It

### Tail Calculations

- **Volume**: Modeled as cone frustum (truncated cone) based on base diameter, tip diameter, and length
- **Dimensions**: Scaled from real fox anatomy (~38% of body length for Red Fox, with species variations)
- **Weight**: Uses realistic fur/tissue density (~1.05 g/cm³) adjusted by species fluff factor
- **Multi-tail Physics**: Weight scaling accounts for the impossibility of supporting 9 full-mass tails

### Body Properties

- **Build Factor**: Age-based maturity curve (0.85–1.15×) plateaus at physical maturity (25 years)
- **Weight**: Cubic scaling from reference height/weight with build adjustments
- **Spiritual Age**: Logarithmic factor representing centuries of accumulated power

### Ears

- **Proportions**: Golden ratio applied to head height for aesthetically perfect fox ears
- **Size**: ~1.4× human ear height, triangular shape approximation

## Interpretation Modes

The Legend Factor slider adjusts the balance between realism and mythology:

- **Field Biologist** (0.0–0.2): Scientifically accurate, fully realistic proportions
- **Folklore Accurate** (0.2–0.45): Grounded in actual folklore with minor embellishments
- **Shrine Canon** (0.45–0.65): Traditional kitsune mythology blend
- **Anime Interpretation** (0.65–0.85): Stylized, visually dramatic proportions
- **Rule-of-Cool** (0.85–1.0): Maximum mythological epicness, physics be damned

## Usage

1. **Set Height**: Enter kitsune height in cm or inches
2. **Choose Age**: From newborn (1) to ancient (9000+ years)
3. **Select Tail Count**: 1–9 tails (one per century of life, traditionally)
4. **Pick Tail Type**: Sleek, Bushy, or Fluffy (affects dimensions and species reference)
5. **Adjust Legend Factor**: Slide between realistic biology and legendary exaggeration
6. **Copy Share Link**: Save and share your exact configuration
