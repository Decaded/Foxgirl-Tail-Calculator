Kitsune Tail Calculator Guide

Unravel the arcane math behind foxgirls: discover how height, age, and those all-important tails shape their mystical stats.

How to Use

Fill in these details to begin your journey:

  • Height (cm): The foxgirl’s standing height, measured in centimeters.
  • Age (years): Her chronological age.
  • Tail Count: Number of tails she currently possesses (1–9).
  • Tail Shape: Choose how sleek or fluffy her tails appear.

How It Works

Behind the scenes, calculations blend biology, spirit lore, and a pinch of fantasy:

  • Body Weight: Based on BMI, adjusted for tails and age.
  • Ear Height: Scales with height, with bonuses from extra tails.
  • Tail Properties: Length, volume, and mass estimated by shape and count.
  • Spirit Power: Grows logarithmically with more tails and age.
  • Foxfire Color: Shifts depending on mystical energy and tail number.

Reminder: All values are fictional, calculated for amusement (and maybe a touch of mischief).
